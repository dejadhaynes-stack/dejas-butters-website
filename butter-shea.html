Description

Experience the gold standard of natural skincare with our premium Shea Butter collection. Sourced from organic shea nuts in West Africa, our unrefined shea butter is the foundation of luxury for melanin-rich skin. Known as "women's gold" in Africa, shea butter has been used for centuries to protect and nourish darker skin tones.

Our Shea Butter is the most versatile base in our collection, offering deep hydration, protection against environmental stressors, and powerful healing properties. Rich in vitamins A, E, and F, it's specifically formulated to address hyperpigmentation, ashiness, and uneven skin tone that commonly affect melanin-rich skin.

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Superior Moisture Retention: Shea butter's fatty acid profile creates a protective barrier that locks in moisture for 24+ hours without white residue on darker skin
  • Fades Hyperpigmentation: Vitamins A and E work together to reduce dark spots, acne scars, and uneven skin tone common in melanin-rich skin
  • Prevents Ashiness: Deep penetrating properties keep darker skin glowing and hydrated, eliminating the ashy appearance
  • Anti-Inflammatory Properties: Natural triterpenes reduce inflammation, soothe eczema, and calm irritated melanin-rich skin
  • Boosts Collagen Production: Vitamin A stimulates collagen synthesis, improving skin elasticity and reducing fine lines
  • Sun Protection: Provides SPF 3-4 natural sun protection, though additional sunscreen is recommended for melanin-rich skin
  • Heals Scars and Stretch Marks: Promotes cell regeneration and improves the appearance of scars and stretch marks on darker skin
  • Non-Comedogenic: Won't clog pores despite its rich texture, making it safe for all skin types including acne-prone melanin-rich skin
