Lavender Dreams Body Butter

$28.99

Description

Drift into tranquility with our Lavender Dreams Body Butter, a luxurious blend crafted specifically for melanin-rich skin. Infused with pure lavender essential oil harvested from organic farms in Provence, France, this deeply nourishing butter melts into your skin like silk, leaving it soft, supple, and beautifully scented.

Perfect for evening routines, this calming formula helps reduce the appearance of dark spots and hyperpigmentation while promoting relaxation and better sleep. The combination of shea butter, coconut oil, and lavender creates the ultimate self-care experience for your mind, body, and soul.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Organic Lavender Essential Oil (Lavandula angustifolia)
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil
  • Organic Cocoa Butter
  • Arrowroot Powder (for smooth absorption)

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Reduces Hyperpigmentation: Lavender oil's natural compounds help fade dark spots and even out skin tone over time
  • Deep Hydration: Shea butter penetrates deeply to moisturize without leaving a greasy residue or white cast on darker skin
  • Anti-Inflammatory: Soothes irritated skin, eczema, and reduces redness - perfect for sensitive melanin-rich skin
  • Promotes Cell Regeneration: Vitamin E and essential fatty acids help repair damaged skin cells and improve elasticity
  • Prevents Ashiness: Long-lasting moisture keeps skin glowing and hydrated for 24+ hours
  • Aromatherapy Benefits: Lavender scent reduces stress, promotes relaxation, and improves sleep quality
  • Antioxidant Protection: Protects melanin-rich skin from environmental stressors and premature aging
  • Non-Comedogenic: Won't clog pores, making it safe for all skin types including acne-prone skin

💚 How to Use

Daily Routine: Apply a small amount (dime to quarter-sized) to slightly damp skin after showering. Massage in circular motions, focusing on dry areas, elbows, knees, and any areas with hyperpigmentation. Use morning and night for best results.

Evening Ritual: Apply before bed as part of your nighttime self-care routine. The lavender scent will help you relax and prepare for restful sleep while the butter works overnight to repair and nourish your skin.
