💚 How to Use

Daily Application: Apply generously to clean, slightly damp skin after bathing. The water helps the butter spread more easily and locks in extra moisture. Focus on dry areas like elbows, knees, and areas prone to hyperpigmentation.

Pro Tip: For maximum absorption, warm a small amount between your palms before applying. This melts the butter slightly and allows it to penetrate deeper into melanin-rich skin. Use morning and night for best results in fading dark spots and maintaining an even, glowing complexion.
