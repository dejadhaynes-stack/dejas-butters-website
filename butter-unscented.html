Unscented Pure Body Butter

$26.99

Description

Experience the purest form of skincare with our Unscented Pure Body Butter, designed specifically for sensitive melanin-rich skin. This fragrance-free formula delivers all the nourishing benefits of our signature butters without any added scents, making it perfect for those with allergies, sensitivities, or who simply prefer unscented products.

Let the natural, subtle aroma of organic shea and cocoa butter soothe your senses while deeply hydrating your skin. Ideal for pregnant women, those with eczema or psoriasis, or anyone who wants pure, clean moisture without overpowering fragrances. This gentle formula fights hyperpigmentation and ashiness while respecting your skin's natural balance.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Organic Cocoa Butter
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Sweet Almond Oil
  • Arrowroot Powder (for smooth absorption)

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Hypoallergenic Formula: Free from essential oils and fragrances, perfect for sensitive melanin-rich skin prone to irritation
  • Maximum Moisture Retention: Pure butters and oils penetrate deeply without white cast, keeping darker skin hydrated for 24+ hours
  • Reduces Hyperpigmentation: Vitamin E and natural fatty acids help fade dark spots and even out skin tone over time
  • Soothes Eczema & Psoriasis: Gentle, anti-inflammatory properties calm flare-ups without irritating sensitive areas
  • Pregnancy Safe: Free from essential oils that may be contraindicated during pregnancy, safe for expecting mothers
  • Prevents Ashiness: Rich emollients create a protective barrier that keeps melanin-rich skin glowing and smooth
  • Improves Skin Barrier: Strengthens your skin's natural defenses against environmental damage and moisture loss
  • Non-Comedogenic: Won't clog pores despite rich texture, suitable for acne-prone melanin-rich skin

💚 How to Use

Daily Application: Apply generously to clean, slightly damp skin after bathing. The water helps the butter spread more easily and locks in extra moisture. Focus on dry areas like elbows, knees, and areas prone to hyperpigmentation.

Layering Tip: Perfect as a base layer under your favorite perfume or scented lotion. The unscented formula won't interfere with other fragrances. Also ideal for use before medical appointments or in scent-free environments.
