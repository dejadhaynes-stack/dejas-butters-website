Citrus Burst Body Butter

Description

Energize your skin and spirit with our Citrus Burst Body Butter, a vibrant blend of sweet orange, lemon, and grapefruit essential oils designed to awaken melanin-rich skin. This refreshing formula delivers a powerful dose of Vitamin C to brighten and even out skin tone while the uplifting citrus scent jumpstarts your day.

Perfect for morning routines, this butter absorbs quickly while providing intense hydration and natural brightening properties that work beautifully on darker skin tones. The combination of citrus oils and nourishing butters helps fade hyperpigmentation, boost collagen production, and leave your skin with a radiant, healthy glow that lasts all day.

  • Lemon Essential Oil (Citrus limon)
  • Grapefruit Essential Oil (Citrus paradisi)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Brightens Skin Tone: High Vitamin C content naturally lightens dark spots and evens out hyperpigmentation on melanin-rich skin
  • Boosts Collagen: Citrus oils stimulate collagen production, improving skin elasticity and reducing the appearance of fine lines
  • Detoxifying Properties: Helps eliminate toxins and impurities, promoting clearer, healthier-looking skin
  • Energizing Moisture: Provides 24-hour hydration without greasy residue, keeping darker skin smooth and radiant
  • Fights Free Radicals: Powerful antioxidants protect melanin-rich skin from environmental damage and premature aging
  • Uplifting Aromatherapy: Citrus scent boosts mood, increases energy, and reduces stress and anxiety
  • Prevents Ashiness: Deep penetrating oils maintain moisture balance, preventing the dull, ashy appearance on dark skin

💚 How to Use

Morning Energizer: Apply after your morning shower to wake up your skin and senses. The invigorating citrus scent pairs perfectly with your morning routine and provides an energy boost to start your day feeling refreshed and confident.

Brightening Treatment: For targeted brightening of dark spots and hyperpigmentation, apply to affected areas twice daily. Consistent use will reveal more even-toned, radiant skin. Always follow with SPF during daytime use.
