Description

Discover the timeless beauty secret of the Mediterranean with our premium Extra Virgin Olive Oil collection. Cold-pressed from organic olives grown in sun-drenched groves, our olive oil butter has been trusted for thousands of years to nourish and protect skin. This ancient beauty staple is particularly beloved for its gentle yet powerful benefits on melanin-rich skin.

Rich in squalene, vitamins E and K, and powerful antioxidants, our olive oil butter provides deep nourishment while protecting melanin-rich skin from premature aging. Unlike synthetic moisturizers, olive oil closely mimics your skin's natural sebum, making it easily absorbed without clogging pores. The silky, luxurious formula melts into darker skin tones, leaving them soft, radiant, and beautifully hydrated with no greasy residue or white cast.

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Anti-Aging Powerhouse: High in squalene and vitamin E, olive oil reduces fine lines and protects melanin-rich skin from premature aging
  • Natural Skin Mimic: Closely resembles skin's natural oils, making it easily absorbed into melanin-rich skin without clogging pores
  • Fades Hyperpigmentation: Vitamin K helps reduce dark circles, age spots, and hyperpigmentation common in darker skin tones
  • Antioxidant Protection: Polyphenols and hydroxytyrosol protect melanin-rich skin from environmental damage and UV exposure
  • Deep Hydration: Fatty acids penetrate deeply to moisturize from within, preventing ashiness and dryness on darker skin
  • Gentle on Sensitive Skin: Naturally hypoallergenic, perfect for melanin-rich skin prone to irritation or allergies
  • Promotes Healing: Anti-inflammatory properties accelerate healing of scars, cuts, and blemishes on darker skin
  • Improves Skin Elasticity: Essential fatty acids and squalene boost collagen production, firming and toning melanin-rich skin

💚 Our Promise

Every jar of Deja's Butters is handcrafted with love by a Black woman in small batches to ensure the highest quality. We use only certified organic, extra virgin olive oil from first cold-press, sourced from family-owned groves in Greece and Spain. Our products are formulated with darker skin tones in mind, addressing concerns like hyperpigmentation, aging, and ashiness while bringing out your natural glow. We never test on animals and are committed to sustainable and ethical sourcing practices. Our products are free from parabens, sulfates, synthetic fragrances, and artificial colors.
