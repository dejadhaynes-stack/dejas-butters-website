Eucalyptus Mint Body Butter

$28.99

Description

Refresh and revitalize your melanin-rich skin with our Eucalyptus Mint Body Butter, a cooling, therapeutic blend that soothes both body and mind. Infused with pure eucalyptus and peppermint essential oils, this invigorating formula provides deep hydration while delivering a refreshing, spa-like experience with every application.

Perfect for post-workout recovery or when you need relief from muscle tension, this butter combines the healing properties of eucalyptus with the cooling sensation of mint. The anti-inflammatory benefits work wonders on melanin-rich skin, helping to reduce irritation, fade dark spots, and promote healing while leaving you feeling refreshed and rejuvenated.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Organic Cocoa Butter
  • Eucalyptus Essential Oil (Eucalyptus globulus)
  • Peppermint Essential Oil (Mentha piperita)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Muscle Relief: Eucalyptus and mint provide natural pain relief for sore muscles and joints, perfect after workouts or long days
  • Anti-Inflammatory Power: Reduces skin inflammation, soothes eczema flare-ups, and calms irritated melanin-rich skin
  • Improved Circulation: Stimulates blood flow to promote healthier, more radiant skin and faster healing of dark spots
  • Deep Hydration: Rich butters penetrate deeply without white cast, keeping darker skin moisturized and preventing ashiness
  • Respiratory Benefits: Eucalyptus aroma helps clear sinuses and improve breathing while you moisturize
  • Cooling Sensation: Natural menthol provides a refreshing, tingly feeling that soothes and energizes skin
  • Antibacterial Properties: Helps prevent breakouts and skin infections, promoting clearer, healthier melanin-rich skin
  • Mental Clarity: Invigorating scent improves focus, reduces mental fatigue, and boosts energy levels

💚 How to Use

Post-Workout Recovery: Apply generously to clean skin after exercise, focusing on sore muscles and joints. The cooling sensation and anti-inflammatory properties help reduce muscle soreness and promote faster recovery.

Morning Revitalizer: Use during your morning routine for an energizing boost. The refreshing scent helps wake you up and prepare you for the day ahead. Avoid applying near eyes or sensitive areas due to the cooling effect.
