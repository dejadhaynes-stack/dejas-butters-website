Coffee Awakening Sugar Scrub

$35.99

Description

Awaken your skin and senses with our Coffee Awakening Sugar Scrub, a powerhouse exfoliating treatment that delivers transformative results for melanin-rich skin. Made with organic Arabica coffee grounds and fine sugar crystals, this invigorating scrub buffs away dead skin cells while the caffeine works to reduce the appearance of cellulite, firm skin, and boost circulation for a radiant, healthy glow.

Coffee is a game-changer for darker skin tones - it brightens hyperpigmentation, tightens pores, and delivers powerful antioxidants that protect melanin-rich skin from premature aging. The energizing coffee aroma stimulates your mind while the scrub stimulates your skin, improving texture and tone. Perfect for reducing the appearance of stretch marks, cellulite, and scarring. This is our most intensive scrub, designed to deliver professional-level results right in your shower.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Arabica Coffee Grounds (Fresh-Ground)
  • Organic Cane Sugar (Fine Grain)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Organic Sweet Almond Oil
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Coffee Seed Oil (Rich in Caffeine)
  • Organic Cocoa Butter

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Reduces Cellulite: Caffeine stimulates blood flow and breaks down fat deposits, visibly reducing the appearance of cellulite
  • Brightens Skin Tone: Coffee's natural acids help fade hyperpigmentation, dark spots, and acne scars on melanin-rich skin
  • Firms and Tightens: Caffeine constricts blood vessels, temporarily tightening skin and reducing puffiness for a more toned appearance
  • Intensive Exfoliation: Dual action of coffee grounds and sugar crystals removes dead skin more effectively than single-agent scrubs
  • Anti-Aging Powerhouse: Loaded with antioxidants that fight free radicals and protect darker skin from premature aging
  • Fades Stretch Marks: Regular use improves the appearance of stretch marks by promoting collagen production and evening skin tone
  • Boosts Circulation: Massaging action combined with caffeine increases blood flow, promoting healthier, more radiant melanin-rich skin
  • Energizing Aromatherapy: Rich coffee aroma boosts mental alertness, increases energy, and enhances mood

💚 How to Use

Cellulite Treatment: Use 3-4 times weekly on areas prone to cellulite (thighs, buttocks, stomach). Apply to wet skin and massage vigorously in circular motions for 5-10 minutes. The caffeine needs time to penetrate, so don't rush. Rinse thoroughly. Consistent use yields best results.

Full Body Glow: For overall skin brightening and exfoliation, use 2-3 times weekly all over your body. The coffee grounds provide more intensive exfoliation than our other scrubs, making it ideal for rough areas and stubborn hyperpigmentation. Follow with body butter to seal in moisture. Note: May temporarily stain light-colored towels.
