Citrus Glow Sugar Scrub

$34.99

Description

Brighten and energize your melanin-rich skin with our Citrus Glow Sugar Scrub, a vitamin-packed exfoliating treatment that reveals your natural radiance. Infused with a uplifting blend of orange, lemon, and grapefruit essential oils, this scrub buffs away dead skin cells while delivering powerful brightening benefits specifically formulated for darker skin tones.

The natural Vitamin C in citrus oils works to fade hyperpigmentation, dark spots, and acne scars while organic sugar crystals gently exfoliate without causing irritation. This refreshing scrub not only improves skin texture but also boosts circulation and collagen production, leaving your melanin-rich skin smoother, brighter, and absolutely glowing. The energizing citrus scent transforms your shower into a spa-like escape.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Cane Sugar (Fine Grain)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Sweet Orange Essential Oil (Citrus sinensis)
  • Lemon Essential Oil (Citrus limon)
  • Grapefruit Essential Oil (Citrus paradisi)
  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Brightens Complexion: Vitamin C from citrus oils naturally lightens dark spots and evens out hyperpigmentation on melanin-rich skin
  • Gentle Exfoliation: Fine sugar crystals remove dead skin without harsh abrasion, revealing smooth, glowing skin underneath
  • Boosts Collagen: Citrus oils stimulate collagen production to improve elasticity and reduce fine lines and wrinkles
  • Fights Acne Scars: Natural acids help fade post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation from acne common in darker skin
  • Detoxifies Skin: Grapefruit oil removes toxins and impurities, promoting clearer, healthier-looking melanin-rich skin
  • Prevents Ingrown Hairs: Regular exfoliation lifts trapped hairs and smooths skin to prevent bumps after shaving
  • Energizing Aromatherapy: Uplifting citrus scent boosts mood, reduces stress, and increases energy levels
  • Enhances Absorption: Removes dead skin layer so moisturizers penetrate deeper, maximizing the benefits of your body butter

💚 How to Use

Morning Glow Ritual: Use 2-3 times weekly in the shower on wet skin. Massage in circular motions, paying special attention to areas with dark spots or rough texture. Rinse with warm water and follow immediately with body butter to seal in moisture and enhance brightening effects.

Targeted Treatment: For stubborn dark spots or hyperpigmentation, gently exfoliate those areas 3-4 times weekly. The vitamin C and natural acids will help fade discoloration over time. Always follow with SPF during daytime as exfoliation increases sun sensitivity.
