Unscented Natural Sugar Scrub

$30.99

Description

Experience pure, gentle exfoliation with our Unscented Natural Sugar Scrub, designed for sensitive melanin-rich skin that deserves effective care without fragrances. This hypoallergenic formula combines fine organic sugar crystals with nourishing organic oils to buff away dead skin cells, reduce hyperpigmentation, and reveal smooth, glowing skin underneath.

Perfect for those with allergies, sensitivities, pregnant women, or anyone who prefers unscented products, this gentle scrub delivers all the benefits of exfoliation without essential oils or fragrances. The natural, subtle aroma of organic butters and oils is all you'll experience while the formula works to even out skin tone, prevent ingrown hairs, and maintain your skin's natural moisture barrier. Ideal for use in scent-free environments or as a base before applying your favorite perfume.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Cane Sugar (Fine Grain)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil
  • Organic Sweet Almond Oil
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Cocoa Butter
  • Arrowroot Powder (for smooth texture)

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Gentle Exfoliation: Fine sugar crystals remove dead skin cells without irritation, perfect for sensitive melanin-rich skin
  • Hypoallergenic Formula: Free from essential oils and fragrances, safe for allergies, sensitivities, and pregnancy
  • Fades Hyperpigmentation: Regular exfoliation speeds up cell turnover, helping to fade dark spots and even skin tone
  • Prevents Ingrown Hairs: Ideal for exfoliating before and after shaving to prevent razor bumps and ingrown hairs
  • Deep Moisturization: Nourishing oils prevent dryness and ashiness after exfoliation, keeping darker skin hydrated
  • Improves Texture: Smooths rough patches and creates even, soft skin surface across all areas of the body
  • Non-Comedogenic: Won't clog pores despite rich oils, suitable for acne-prone melanin-rich skin
  • Fragrance-Free Versatility: Perfect base for layering with perfumes or use in scent-sensitive environments

💚 How to Use

Sensitive Skin Routine: Use 2-3 times weekly on wet skin in the shower. Apply gently in circular motions - there's no need for aggressive scrubbing. The fine sugar crystals are effective without harsh pressure. Focus on areas with hyperpigmentation, rough texture, or dryness. Rinse thoroughly.

Pre-Shaving Prep: Exfoliate 24 hours before shaving or waxing to lift trapped hairs and prevent ingrown hairs. The gentle formula won't irritate sensitive areas. Can also be used on face for gentle facial exfoliation. Follow with our Unscented Pure Body Butter for complete fragrance-free skincare routine.
