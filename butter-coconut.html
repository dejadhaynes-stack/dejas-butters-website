Description

Transport yourself to tropical paradise with our Virgin Coconut Oil collection. Cold-pressed from fresh coconut meat within hours of harvest, our organic coconut oil retains all of its natural vitamins, minerals, and fatty acids. This lightweight yet deeply nourishing oil has been used for centuries in tropical regions to protect and beautify melanin-rich skin.

Unlike heavy butters, coconut oil absorbs quickly into darker skin tones without leaving a greasy residue or white cast. Its unique molecular structure allows it to penetrate deeply, delivering moisture where it's needed most. Rich in lauric acid and vitamin E, our coconut oil butter helps brighten dark spots, smooth skin texture, and maintain that coveted natural glow on melanin-rich skin.

💚 Our Promise

Every jar of Deja's Butters is handcrafted with love by a Black woman in small batches to ensure the highest quality.
