Lavender Calm Sugar Scrub

$32.99

Description

Drift into serenity while revealing smooth, radiant skin with our Lavender Calm Sugar Scrub. This soothing exfoliating treatment combines fine organic sugar crystals with pure French lavender essential oil to gently buff away dead skin cells while calming both body and mind. Specially formulated for melanin-rich skin, this scrub addresses hyperpigmentation while providing a tranquil, spa-like experience.

Perfect for evening self-care rituals, this gentle scrub not only exfoliates but also delivers anti-inflammatory benefits that soothe irritated skin, reduce redness, and promote healing. The calming lavender aroma helps reduce stress and prepare you for restful sleep while the nourishing oils ensure your skin stays hydrated and glowing, never ashy. Follow with our Lavender Dreams Body Butter for the ultimate relaxation experience.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Cane Sugar (Fine Grain)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Organic Lavender Essential Oil (Lavandula angustifolia)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Sweet Almond Oil
  • Dried Lavender Buds (for gentle exfoliation)

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Gentle Exfoliation: Fine sugar crystals remove dead skin cells without irritation, revealing smooth, glowing melanin-rich skin
  • Fades Dark Spots: Lavender's natural compounds help reduce hyperpigmentation and even out skin tone over time
  • Anti-Inflammatory Relief: Soothes eczema, psoriasis, and other inflammatory conditions common in sensitive melanin-rich skin
  • Prevents Ingrown Hairs: Regular exfoliation lifts trapped hairs and smooths skin, reducing bumps after shaving or waxing
  • Promotes Healing: Lavender oil accelerates healing of scars, wounds, and blemishes while preventing infection
  • Stress Relief: Calming lavender aroma reduces anxiety, promotes relaxation, and improves sleep quality
  • Moisturizing Formula: Nourishing oils prevent dryness after exfoliation, keeping darker skin hydrated and preventing ashiness
  • Improves Texture: Regular use smooths rough patches and creates soft, touchable skin with an even surface

💚 How to Use

Evening Ritual: Use 2-3 times weekly during your evening shower or bath. Apply to wet skin and massage gently in circular motions, taking deep breaths to inhale the calming lavender aroma. Focus on rough areas and zones with hyperpigmentation. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry.

Self-Care Sunday: Transform your weekly exfoliation into a mindful self-care ritual. Light candles, play relaxing music, and take your time massaging the scrub into your skin. This is your moment to unwind and show your melanin-rich skin the love it deserves. Follow with Lavender Dreams Body Butter for maximum relaxation and hydration.
