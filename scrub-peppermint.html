Peppermint Refresh Sugar Scrub

$33.99

Description

Awaken your senses and reveal radiant melanin-rich skin with our Peppermint Refresh Sugar Scrub. This invigorating exfoliating treatment combines fine organic sugar crystals with cooling peppermint essential oil to buff away dead skin cells, reduce hyperpigmentation, and leave your skin incredibly soft and glowing.

Specially formulated for darker skin tones, this scrub gently removes the dull, ashy layer that can make melanin-rich skin appear lackluster. The peppermint provides a refreshing, tingly sensation while stimulating circulation to promote even skin tone and a natural glow. Follow with our body butter for the ultimate self-care experience that leaves your skin smooth, bright, and deeply moisturized.

🌿 Pure Ingredients

  • Organic Cane Sugar (Fine Grain)
  • Organic Coconut Oil (Virgin, Cold-Pressed)
  • Organic Shea Butter (Unrefined)
  • Peppermint Essential Oil (Mentha piperita)
  • Organic Sweet Almond Oil
  • Vitamin E Oil (Natural Tocopherol)
  • Organic Jojoba Oil
  • Peppermint Leaf Extract

✨ Proven Benefits for Melanin-Rich Skin

  • Exfoliates Dead Skin: Fine sugar crystals gently remove dead skin cells and dull layer without irritating melanin-rich skin
  • Reduces Hyperpigmentation: Regular exfoliation helps fade dark spots, acne scars, and uneven skin tone faster
  • Prevents Ingrown Hairs: Excellent for exfoliating before and after shaving or waxing to prevent ingrown hairs common in darker skin
  • Improves Circulation: Peppermint stimulates blood flow, promoting healthier, more radiant-looking skin
  • Cooling Sensation: Natural menthol provides a refreshing, invigorating experience that energizes your routine
  • Deep Cleansing: Removes impurities from pores without stripping natural oils, preventing ashiness after exfoliation
  • Enhances Product Absorption: Removes dead skin layer so moisturizers and treatments penetrate deeper into melanin-rich skin
  • Aromatherapy Benefits: Peppermint scent boosts energy, improves focus, and relieves mental fatigue

💚 How to Use

In the Shower: Apply to wet skin in the shower or bath. Massage in gentle circular motions, focusing on rough areas like elbows, knees, and heels. Use 2-3 times per week for best results. Rinse thoroughly with warm water and pat dry.

Pre-Shaving Prep: Exfoliate 24 hours before shaving or waxing to prevent ingrown hairs. The scrub lifts trapped hairs and smooths skin for a closer, cleaner shave. Follow with our body butter to lock in moisture and maintain your glow. Avoid using on broken skin or immediately after shaving.
